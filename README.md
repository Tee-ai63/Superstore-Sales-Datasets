# Superstore Sales & Profitability Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 4 years (2014–2017) of Superstore 
retail sales data to identify profitability drivers, underperforming 
product lines, and regional sales patterns.

## Business Problem
Where is the business truly profitable, and where is it quietly losing 
money despite strong sales?

## Key Insights
- Furniture generates nearly as much revenue as Office Supplies but earns 
  8x less profit
- Tables and Bookcases operate at a net loss, driven by aggressive discounting
- Discounting alone doesn't predict losses — Binders proves high discount 
  can coexist with strong profit
- Sales peak seasonally in November, informing inventory/staffing planning

## Dashboard Features
- 4 KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Profit Margin
- Category & Sub-Category profitability breakdown
- Geographic sales map by state
- Monthly sales trend line
- Discount vs. Profit scatter analysis
- Interactive Region and Date-range slicers

## Tools Used
Power BI Desktop, DAX (SUM, DIVIDE, DISTINCTCOUNT)
